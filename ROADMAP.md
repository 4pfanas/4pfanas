# Green Chart Roadmap

A 12-month plan (Sep 2026 to Sep 2027) to fill the contribution graph with real work. The target is **250+ active days**, about 5 a day-slots a week. Each phase below is a [milestone](https://github.com/4pfanas/4pfanas/milestones), and each task is an [issue](https://github.com/4pfanas/4pfanas/issues).

| Phase | Months | Active days | Running total |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Foundation | Sep to Oct 2026 | 15 | 15 |
| 2. Publish the backlog | Nov to Dec 2026 | 40 | 55 |
| 3. Build the teaching site | Jan to Mar 2027 | 60 | 115 |
| 4. Contribute to others | Apr to Jun 2027 | 65 | 180 |
| 5. Polish and maintain | Jul to Sep 2027 | 70 | 250 |

## What counts on the graph

Commits to the default branch made with a verified email, opened pull requests, opened issues and submitted reviews. Local commits that are never pushed do not count.

## Weekly rhythm

Mon: write the week's task as an issue. Tue and Wed: build. Thu: test and close. Fri: docs. Sat: one `learning-python` exercise. Sun: rest, or review one pull request.

## Rules

- Real work only. No empty or fake commits.
- One issue per task. Close it with the commit.
- Never commit `node_modules`, `.env`, keys or `.DS_Store`.

The interactive version, with checklists, lives in the Green Chart Roadmap artifact.
